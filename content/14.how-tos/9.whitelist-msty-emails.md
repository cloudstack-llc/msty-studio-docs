Msty Studio sends important account and access emails from:

```txt
Msty Studio <hello@notifications.msty.studio>
```

These emails can include license key messages, login links, and Enterprise access codes. If you do not receive one of these emails, use the steps below before requesting a new code or contacting support.

## Check Your Inbox

::steps

1. Confirm the email address entered in Msty Studio is correct.
2. Check your **Junk**, **Spam**, **Promotions**, **Quarantine**, or **Clutter** folders.
3. Search your mailbox for `hello@notifications.msty.studio` or `Msty Studio`.
4. If you find the email outside your inbox, mark it as **Not Spam** or move it to your inbox.

::

## Ask IT to Whitelist Msty Studio

For Enterprise users, company email filters may block, quarantine, or delay access-code emails before they reach the user's mailbox.

Security-conscious organizations may need to whitelist Msty Studio on their side before these emails can be delivered. This is especially common for education institutions and other organizations with strict email security protocols.

Ask your IT or email administration team to check whether messages from Msty Studio are being blocked, quarantined, trapped, or delayed by:

- Email security gateways
- Spam filters
- Mail-flow rules
- Quarantine policies
- Phishing or impersonation protection tools

Your IT team should whitelist or allowlist this sender:

```txt
hello@notifications.msty.studio
```

If possible, allow messages from the `notifications.msty.studio` sending domain as well.

## If Emails Still Do Not Arrive

If the email is not in junk or quarantine and IT confirms the sender is not blocked, contact Msty:

```txt
hello@msty.ai
```

Include the email address that should receive the message and whether you are waiting for a license key, login link, or Enterprise access code.
