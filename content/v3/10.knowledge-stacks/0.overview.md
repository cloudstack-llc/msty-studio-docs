A Knowledge Stack turns documents and other sources into searchable context. Use it when a model needs to answer from your material.

![A saved Knowledge Stack with a note source, ready to compose](/images/v3/knowledge-stack.png)

## Create and compose

1. Open **Knowledge Stacks** and choose **New Knowledge Stack**.
2. Enter a title and, optionally, a description.
3. Add content under **Files**, **Folders**, **Notes**, **YouTube Links**, **Web Links**, **Conversations**, or **Conversation Projects**.
4. Open **Compose Settings** to choose an available embedding model and review how content is divided into chunks.
5. Choose **Compose** and wait for the stack to become ready.

**Save** stores changes to the stack. **Compose** prepares its content for search. A saved stack without a ready index cannot be added to a conversation.

## Use it in a conversation

Open the conversation's **Knowledge Stacks** add-on and select a ready stack. You can also attach stacks to a project or persona for repeated use.

Ask a question that the selected sources can answer. For example, after composing your project notes, ask: “Which tasks are still open?”

See [Add sources](/v3/knowledge-stacks/sources) and [Query Settings](/v3/knowledge-stacks/query-settings) for more control.
