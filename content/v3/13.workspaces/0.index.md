title: Workspaces and projects
description: Separate your work and organize related conversations.
---

A workspace keeps a separate set of local work. Projects organize conversations inside a workspace.

## Create a workspace

1. Open the workspace selector in the sidebar.
2. Choose **New Workspace**.
3. Enter a name and choose a color.
4. On desktop, optionally choose the folder where Msty should store the workspace.
5. Choose **Create and Switch**, or use **Create and Stay** to keep working in the current workspace.

Switch between workspaces from the same selector. Its actions include editing, exporting, importing, and workspace protection.

## Organize conversations with projects

Create a project from the conversation sidebar, then move related conversations into it. A project can provide shared model and add-on choices such as attachments, Knowledge Stacks, toolsets, and Live Contexts.

For example, keep one project for a research topic and attach its reference material there instead of selecting it for every conversation.

## Protect and back up

Workspace protection can lock a workspace with a passphrase. Keep that passphrase somewhere safe and export a backup before changing storage or protection settings.

Use the workspace menu to export one workspace. For a broader backup, see [Back up and restore data](/v3/settings/data-backups).

Workspaces are local; creating one does not synchronize it to another device.
