title: Model Matchmaker
description: Rank models against the strengths and tradeoffs that matter for your task.
---

Model Matchmaker helps create a shortlist. It does not install a model or measure its performance on your computer.

## Compare models

1. Open **Model Matchmaker**.
2. Choose **Local**, **Remote**, or both model types to compare.
3. Adjust the strength sliders for priorities such as coding, reasoning, language, context, or token cost.
4. Review the ranked models. Select a model name to inspect its strength chart, or open its capabilities.
5. Connect or install a shortlisted model, then test it with your own prompts.

Save a strength preset if you regularly compare models for the same kind of work.

Use [Online Providers](/v3/managing-models/online-providers) or [Local Models](/v3/managing-models/local-models) to make a chosen model available in conversations.
