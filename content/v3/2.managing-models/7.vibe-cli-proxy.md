---
title: Vibe CLI Proxy
description: Connect supported coding assistant accounts and use their available models in Msty Studio.
---

Vibe CLI Proxy runs a local service that connects supported coding assistant accounts to Msty Studio. After you authorize an account, its available models can appear in the conversation model selector.

## Connect an account

1. Open **Settings > Vibe CLI Proxy** in Msty Studio Desktop.
2. Install the proxy and review and accept the notice shown in the app.
3. Under **Authentication**, open the menu for a supported provider and choose **Authorize**.
4. Complete the provider's browser sign-in or device-code flow.
5. With the proxy running, open a conversation and select an available model. Msty discovers and synchronizes the connected models automatically.

Available providers and models depend on the proxy version and your account. Follow your provider's terms for the account and models you use.

## Manage the proxy

Return to **Settings > Vibe CLI Proxy** to start or stop the service, manage authentication, review logs, or change its port and network settings. The proxy runs locally, while requests to connected models go to their providers.
